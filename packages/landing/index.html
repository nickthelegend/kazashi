01 · Method

How it works

A virtual pinhole lantern projects your bare hands onto the plaster.

Three physical steps take you from an empty desk to living shadow theatre.

  1. 01 Place the lamp: Set the virtual paper lantern on your desk facing any blank wall.
  2. 02 Cast the shadow: Hold a shape in the lantern light until it inks.
  3. 03 Direct the play: Match creature silhouettes to ink living performers that leap your room.
