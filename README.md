# Kazashi (翳し)

*"Holding your hand up to the light."*

Hold your hands up to a paper lantern on your desk. Their shadow falls on your real wall. Shape a dog, and when the
shadow matches, it inks into a living creature that runs along your wall. Collect the cast, then perform a 20-second
shadow play with them.

Meta VR Start 2026 — Entertainment track, New Experience division, IWSDK (WebXR). Spec: `grokbot/specs/meta-vr/kazashi.md`.
