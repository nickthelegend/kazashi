# kazashi art direction

Generated with ChatGPT's image model (via the Codex image tool on a ChatGPT plan). Source PNGs are kept out of git; the app ships WebP copies.

- **Style and palette:** sepia shadow-theatre illustration with warm lamp light and crisp hand-shadow silhouettes on textured plaster, palette sepia #6b4f36, warm lamp #ffb35c, cream #f7ecd9, deep brown #241a12.
- **Banned:** real brands or logos, readable text, real people, other designers' artwork.
- **Files:** `packages/landing/public/art/`
